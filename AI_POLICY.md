# AI-Assisted Contributions Policy

HDCharts welcomes AI tools that help contributors develop changes. Fully autonomous pull requests are not accepted. Every contribution remains the responsibility of the person who submits it: automated tools do not replace review, understanding, or communication with maintainers.

## Contributor responsibilities

1. **Review and verify changes.** The pull-request author must personally review, understand, and test every submitted change. They must be able to explain the change, its behavior, and the validation they ran.
2. **Participate in code review.** Contributors must respond to review feedback in their own words. Do not copy maintainer comments into an AI tool and paste an unverified response into the review thread.
3. **Coordinate larger changes.** Discuss features, broad refactorings, public API changes, and cross-module work in an [issue](https://github.com/HDCharts/charts/issues) before implementation. When maintainers request a design or decision record, agree on it before opening the implementation pull request.

AI assistance is compatible with these responsibilities when the contributor remains accountable for the submitted work.
